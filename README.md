# Temple of Abyss

## Overview
[cite_start]**Temple of Abyss** is a 3D adventure, puzzle, and exploration game developed in Unity3D[cite: 1, 15, 17]. [cite_start]Players assume the role of a courageous archaeologist navigating an ancient, trap-filled temple to retrieve the "Golden Heart of the Abyss" and escape alive[cite: 29, 31].

## Gameplay Features
* [cite_start]**Exploration & Puzzles:** Navigate five distinct levels filled with lever puzzles, pressure plates, and torch-lighting sequences[cite: 15, 61, 62, 116].
* [cite_start]**Physics-Based Traps:** Interact with deadly environment hazards like spike floors, swinging blades, falling boulders, and fire jets, all driven by Unity’s physics engine[cite: 53, 54, 79].
* [cite_start]**Inventory & Checkpoints:** Manage keys, artifacts, and power-ups using an on-screen inventory system [cite: 83, 128][cite_start], and utilize checkpoint orbs to save progress[cite: 90].
* [cite_start]**Secret Rooms:** Discover hidden passages containing rare artifacts and bonus loot to increase your total score[cite: 73, 86, 87].
* [cite_start]**Power-Up System:** Utilize Speed Boosts, Trap Immunity, and Health Potions to survive the temple's depths[cite: 94, 95, 96].
